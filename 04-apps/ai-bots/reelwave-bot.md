# ReelWave Bot

Helps users make vertical micro-dramas.

## What it does

- Suggests characters, scenes, props, hooks
- Builds index-card decks (max 100 cards, ≤500 chars each)
- Helps title the project
- Writes short episode prompts for the AI Studio
- Posts ideas to community feed / groups

## Typical flow

1. User: “Make a secret wife CEO drama”
2. Bot: creates title + 8–20 index cards
3. User opens ReelWave Studio → Generate
4. Optional: Post to Community

## Card limit reminder

- Max **100 cards**
- Max **~500 characters** per card
- Always include a **project title**
