## What it is

A creator-owned streaming platform that turns the playful experiments of doaashow (microdrama, radioshow, The Fam, desktop tools) into a clean, cinematic home for audiences.

- **Original Series** — web series & microdramas
- **Audio First** — radio shows, podcasts, narrative audio
- **Community Layer** — The Fam social layer
- **Interactive** — desktop experiments & live events
